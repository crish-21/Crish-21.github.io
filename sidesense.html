SIDESENSE (3B9 Design Project)

The challenge

For our third-year engineering module, my team was tasked with coming up with a practical solution to a real-world issue. After conducting surveys and analyzing current public transport, we identified the area we wanted to focus in: the communication between bus drivers and cyclists at bus stops. We aimed to design a system that would increase situational awareness and reduce the risk of accidents, making both bus drivers and cyclists feel safer and more confident when interacting at bus stops.

My role and technical challenges

For this project, I was in charge of the documenting, presentation and any design-related tasks, overseeing the development of our prototype and creating the final video.

  • My main responsibility was to oversee the construction of a model that balanced aesthetics and functional use. The challenge was creating a structural base that looked realistic while leaving space to add the electronics and sensors without sacrificing structural integrity.
  • I managed the documentation and visual communication. As part of the module we had to create weekly presentations and create a final video to wrap up our work in the project.

The solution

To build a faithful prototype, we used several materials depending on the need. We used high-density foam as our base as they are easy to cut and give shape and are strong enough as a support to the structure. To ensure the prototype accurately represented a real-world bus stop, I used SolidWorks to design a shelter and support pole, which were 3D printed to include in the prototype. During the project, I focused on keeping record of our progress. I kept a log that tracked what worked and how our ideas evolved and why we made certain design changes. Documenting our meetings sessions made it possible to build a clear narrative that showed our engineering process used real data and solved a real-world problem.

Outcome

Our solution was completed by the timeline, and met all the requirements. The final prototype worked perfectly when it was time for our grouop to present. We also submitted our project for a competition, the Smarter Travel Student Awards, earning 3rd place.
This experience taught me the importance of documenting progress during a project, to have a clear map of the overall process and to be able to go back when the project gets into a halt. It also improved my ability to work in a team, organizing and distributing the tasks according to ability, and my ability to create good quality videos and presentations.
