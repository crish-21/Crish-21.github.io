3B7 Manufacturing Technology Project

The Project

For my 3B7 Manufacturing Technology module, our goal was to reverse engineer a pencil sharpener, producing detailed technical drawings, manufacturing instructions and DFMA analysis.

My role and technical challenges

For this project my tasks as the team lead and coordinator can be summarized as follows:

  • I was in charge of managing and distibuting tasks for the group for the different parts of the project.
  • CAD Modeling: My main role was to assist in whatever aspects the other people in the team needed help with, whether it be making the more difficult components or helping with the assembly.
  • CAD Drawing: I was in charge designing a logo for the team and rearranging the parts of the drawing to make it look more professional.
  • Assembly Guide: I was responsible for producing the video showing the assembly in real time, adding the captions and coordinating the design of the assembly guide, as well as producing the CAD images showing each step of the assembly.
  • Material and Manufacturing Methods: This part of the assignment required us to identify the material and manufacturing method for each part. I investigated about the bigger parts, most of them made of plastic.
  • DFMA Analysis: My role was to design and determine how the pencil sharpener could be modified to simplify the manufacture process, material selection and reduce costs. I created a CAD model with alterations from the main design and gave it to the rest of the team, who analized the design in more depth to show numerically that the new design meant an improvement in the different aspects proposed.

The solution

To work with the problems we encountered throughout the project, we came up with several solutions:

  • We discussed each person's strength and weaknesses in the aspects we would need to work on for the project, and took note to distribute the tasks according to their skills.
  • CAD Modeling: We ended up having problems with the complexity of some components and with mates in the assembly, so I decided to take on the CAD models the other team members were having difficulty with since I am more experienced in SolidWorks, and finished the assembly for an accurate representation.
  • CAD Drawing: It was complicated to come up with a logo design that would reflect the team's identity, but after an investigation, I decided to draw a pencil shaving in the loose shape of a pencil. It turned out to be a simple and effective design. The rearranging of components in the drawings was very straight forward and didn't suppose any complications.
  • Assembly Guide: It was challenging to create a clear assembly guide and decide how we wanted it to look. At the end, we decided that we would include CAD drawings indicating each component and clear images along with text and a video instructing the order and how to assemble the pencil sharpener, and use OverLeaf for its flexibility in designing documents.
  • Material and Manufacturing Methods: This part of the project turned out to be more complicated that we thought. The material selection was no problem, but choosing the manufacturing method and explaining our reasons, along with evidence for out reasoning, proved to be difficult, given out limited experience with manufacturing methods and the amount of them that exist. We ended up doing a lot of reasearch together to help each other and come up with a final decision.
  • DFMA Analysis: This part proved to be the most fun and my favourite. I did a lot of reasearch and tested different designs to see which ones could effectively reduce the number of parts, use cheaper materials (basically see what we could replace with plastic), and be easy and straightforward to use and put together. I am very happy with the outcome, and my teammates did a great job with the quantifying analysis of my design.

The outcome

For each part of the project, we were able to produce a final outcome that we wer happy with and that we felt reflected our effort. Overall, our research and modelling in the project was very detailed and well documented, and it reflected in our grades.
In this project I learnt the process of reverse engineering, designing and manufacturing of a product, and the how engineering and reasearch is a necessary step in the design process.I also learnt to work in a team and how to distribute tasks effectively.
